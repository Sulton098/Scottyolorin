THE ARTIST

ABOUT

Scottyolorin is a genre-bending artist whose roots run deep into structured music training; between church choir rehearsals and orchestral performances, he developed a refined sense of emotion, melody and rhythm in childhood years. his sonic journey began in university with BandLab and raw curiosity. He started uploading experimental tracks to SoundCloud, where he quickly gained a following for his unique sound and innovative approach to music production.
